How It Works

The LUMIS method

01
Discovery Call
We begin with a deep conversation about your vision, lifestyle, budget, and timeline. No forms. No briefs. Just a real dialogue.
02
AI Moodboarding
Our AI system generates concept boards in real-time based on your inputs — colour palettes, spatial arrangements, material combinations.
03
Design Development
Senior designers refine the AI concepts into detailed design packages with full technical drawings and specification sheets.
04
Execution & Install
We manage every contractor, supplier, and delivery — so the transformation happens seamlessly, on time and on budget.
